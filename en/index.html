Markets

Five markets, five ways of holding dental data

The product is the same everywhere. What changes is who pays for adult dental care and therefore where the data sits today. In every country, the only source present everywhere is each practice's own software.

SPAIN · PILOT

Private and fragmented

Patients pay 97% of dental spending; records sit in the software of thousands of private practices, chains and insurers. We are looking for a chain or insurer for a quality indicators pilot.

Spanish page →

NETHERLANDS · PARTNERS

Consolidation and shortage

More than 500 practices owned by private equity and about 30% of practices not taking new patients. Chains need to compare practices without migrating records.

Dutch page →

BELGIUM · ALLIANCE

Payer-driven data

Compulsory insurance turns every reimbursable dental act into structured data, but the full record and non-reimbursed care are missing. A market for an institutional alliance.

Nederlands →  Français →

GERMANY · SOFTWARE PARTNERS

Institutional and integrated

The electronic patient record has been mandatory in dental practices since 1 October 2025. The opportunity is data quality, compliance and federated analytics for software vendors.

DENMARK · ALLIANCE

A public register for children

Free care up to age 21 and a children's oral health register since 1972. Adults treated privately and in chains remain largely invisible.

The solution

A federated data space: data never leaves the practice

Practices, chains and insurers keep their records. Only consents, metadata and aggregated results move. The space becomes the natural counterpart of the health data access bodies.

Use cases with recurring revenue

  • Quality indicators across practices of the same group: the first use case, available today.
  • AI on dental images, without moving a single X-ray. Our model for interproximal caries and periodontal bone loss is in clinical validation.
  • Evidence for industry on implants and materials.
  • Never for advertising: the EHDS prohibits it.

The window of opportunity

26/03/2027Every Member State will have designated its health data access bodies
26/03/2029Most secondary use rules apply
2031Medical images are added: X-rays, panoramic radiographs and cone-beam CT

Whoever has a working dental data space in 2029 will be the natural partner of regulators.

Technology and experience

Open European standards, already proven in three real data spaces

A connector on Microsoft Azure, integrated with the practice management software. European health record format and common metadata, adapted to each market.

Sovereignty

The practice decides who gets access, for what and for how long.

ODRL contracts

Usage conditions enforced by the machine. No contract, no access.

Gaia-X and IDSA

Verifiable credentials, Dataspace Protocol, Eclipse EDC, FHIR, DICOM and HealthDCAT-AP.

AI without moving data

Federated learning and confidential computing: the algorithm goes to the data.
